Only numbers leave this page: dBFS, VAD, luminance, frame motion, and device labels. No audio, no frames, no recording. Samples are sent at 5 Hz while a sensor is live and the tab is visible.

Why this page exists

The vendor interface has a face player (RcEmoticonPlayerService/PlayerEmoticon, port 59001, reachable) but no getter for the current expression. Audio status (TTSService/GetAudioStatus) and volume (HalAudioService/GetAudioVolume) live on ports 59301 and 56666, which are not exposed on the Wi-Fi interface. Microphone audio is a ROS 2 topic behind an SSH-only process.

So: the robot can be told to show a face, and can be heard, but it cannot currently report either. These two sensors close that gap physically. Camera and microphone stay in this tab; there is no upload path and no recording.
