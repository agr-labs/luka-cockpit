Voice commands go through the lease and capability gates. Physical Luka is observe-only; select the simulator to execute.
What it can do

The browser recognises speech, maps it to a capability, and sends it through the gateway’s lease and validation gates. Supported: look up/down/left/right/centre, open/close hands, say <text>, listen on/off, status, and stop. On a physical robot a lease in observe mode is read-only; commands are refused by the gateway. Voice never bypasses the e-stop or profile locks.
